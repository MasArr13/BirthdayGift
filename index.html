A Little Note

For your new chapter

I don't really know what the perfect birthday message is supposed to look like, but I know I wanted to make something for you. Maybe it's a little unusual, but I think you deserve a little corner of the internet dedicated to your day.

Looking back, I'm genuinely grateful that our paths crossed. We've shared so many little things together, some probably felt insignificant at the time, but somehow they became memories that stayed with me. The conversations, the random things we talked about, the little moments in between everything else — all of them became a part of a chapter that I'll always be grateful for.

You once told me that I became a turning point for you to become a better person. I don't think you realize how much that means to me. But honestly, you've done something similar for me too. There are things I learned from knowing you that I'll probably carry with me for a very long time. So if there's one thing I hope you remember, it's that the time we spent knowing each other wasn't meaningless. At least for me, it never was.

Some people leave a mark on your life quietly, and you only realize how meaningful it was when you look back.

And now you're entering another year, another chapter, with so many things ahead of you. I hope you get to meet people who appreciate you for who you are, find places where you genuinely feel like you belong, and have enough peace of mind to enjoy the things you're working so hard for.

I hope you keep growing, but I also hope you don't become so focused on becoming "better" that you forget to appreciate who you already are. I hope you chase the things you believe in, even when the road gets difficult. Be stubborn, just like what you told me to be wkwkwk. And when things don't go exactly as planned, I hope you remember that the process itself is still worth living through.

There will probably be a lot of new things waiting for you this year. New places, new people, new challenges, maybe even versions of yourself that you haven't met yet. Whatever they are, I hope they bring you closer to the life that you want.

And most importantly, I hope you'll find happiness in places you don't expect. Not just in achievements or big moments, but in random conversations, late nights, small victories, good food, and all the little things that make an ordinary day feel worth remembering.

You deserve a good year, Gaby. I really hope you have one.
